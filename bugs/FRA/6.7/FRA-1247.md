# FRA-1247: [Android] Zoom does not work in newspapers view

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Rema 1000 app
2. Navigate to the Tilbudsavis (newspapers/flyers) section
3. Open a newspaper/flyer
4. Try to pinch-to-zoom on the newspaper page

## Expected Result
- The newspaper page should zoom in/out smoothly following the pinch gesture

## Actual Result
- The zoom gesture does not respond. The newspaper page remains at its default zoom level and cannot be zoomed in or out.
