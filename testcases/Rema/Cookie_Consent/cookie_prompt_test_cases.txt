cookie_prompt-01 | Verify cookie prompt appears on fresh app install

        User has never installed the app before
        App is freshly installed on device
    Actions
        Launch the app for the first time
        Observe the bottom sheet that appears

        Three action buttons are visible: "Det er OK", "Kun nødvendige", "Indstil præferencer"
        Links "Læs mere om vores persondatapolitik" and "Læs mere" are visible
