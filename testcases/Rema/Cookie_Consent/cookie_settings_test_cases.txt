cookie_settings-01 | Verify accessing cookie settings from More menu
    Actions
        Navigate to More menu and tap cookie settings option
    Expected
        Cookie settings screen opens showing current preferences

cookie_settings-02 | Verify viewing currently accepted cookies
    Actions
        Open cookie settings from More menu
    Expected
        Screen displays "Nødvendige" and "Funktionelle - fejlsøgning" toggles with correct states

cookie_settings-03 | Verify necessary cookies toggle cannot be disabled
    Actions
        Attempt to turn OFF "Nødvendige" toggle
    Expected
        Toggle remains ON and does not respond to tap

cookie_settings-04 | Verify enabling functional cookies
    Actions
        Turn ON "Funktionelle - fejlsøgning" toggle and save
    Expected
        Toggle changes to ON and preference is saved

cookie_settings-05 | Verify disabling functional cookies
    Actions
        Turn OFF "Funktionelle - fejlsøgning" toggle and save
    Expected
        Toggle changes to OFF and preference is saved

cookie_settings-06 | Verify cookie preferences persist after app restart
    Actions
        Change cookie preferences, save, close app, reopen and check settings
    Expected
        Previously saved preferences are maintained

cookie_settings-07 | Verify canceling preference changes
    Actions
        Change toggle state and tap back without saving
    Expected
        Changes are discarded and original preferences remain
