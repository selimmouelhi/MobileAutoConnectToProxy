PICKER_FLOW-01 | Swipe to pick item with quantity 1 - Success flow [P0]
    Preconditions
        User is logged in as a picker
        User has an active job with unpicked items in the Job section
        At least one item has quantity equal to 1
    Actions
        Navigate to the job details screen
        Locate an item with quantity 1 in the Job section
        Swipe the item to pick
        Observe the item state during the API call
        Wait for the request to complete
    Expected
        Loading indicator appears in the job item price area
        Swipe action is disabled during the request
        Item is moved from Job section to Basket section after success
        Item displays correct details in Basket section
        Total area updates to reflect the picked item
    API
        POST v3/jobs/{job-id}/baskets/{basket-id}/items - Body contains only barcode (amount 1 implicit)
        GET v3/jobs/{job-id}/baskets/{basket-id} - Updates basket total after item added
