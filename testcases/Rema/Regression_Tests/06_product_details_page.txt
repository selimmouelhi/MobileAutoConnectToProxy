Open Product Details Page and View Info
    Preconditions
        --User is logged in; product is visible in search, shopping list, or catalog
    Actions
        Tap on any product
    Expected
        Product name, image, description, and base price are displayed
        Labels (e.g., eco, local, age restrictions) are visible
        "Add to list" and "Add to favorites" buttons are shown (if logged in)

Add / Remove product to shopping list
    Actions
        Tap "+" to add item
        Tap "–" to remove item
    Expected
        Item is added/removed from the shopping list
        Count reflects real-time sync with list, as well as favorites if applied
        In app notification is shown and navigates the user to current shopping list 
Add to Favorites/ Remove from favorites
    Preconditions
        --User is logged in
    Actions
        Open any product
        Tap on the heart to favorite an item
        Tap on the enabled heart to disable it 
    Expected
        Product is added in first step to favorites with correct number of items
        Item is removed from favorite cards in front page
        Item is removed from all favorites list
