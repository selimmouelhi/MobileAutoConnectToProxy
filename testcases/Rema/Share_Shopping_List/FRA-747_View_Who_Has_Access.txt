Share Shopping List - FRA-747: View Who Has Access

WHO_ACCESS-01 | Who has access section displays correct title [P0]
    Preconditions
        User is logged in to the Rema 1000 app
        User has navigated to a shopping list
        Share shopping list sheet is presented
    Actions
        Observe the "Who has access" section in the share sheet
        Verify the section title text
    Expected
        The section displays a title matching localization key app_share_list.access_section_title
        The title label is constrained to max 1 line and does not wrap
        The title is clearly visible and not truncated
    API
        GET /api/shopping-lists/{id} - Returns shopping list with shared users data
