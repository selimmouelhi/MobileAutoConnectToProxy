SHARE_LIST-01 | Unshared list displays "Del liste" button instead of avatars [P0]
    Preconditions
        User is logged in to the Rema 1000 app
        User has navigated to a shopping list that is not shared with any other user
        Shopping list data has been fully fetched
    Actions
        Observe the share area on the shopping list screen
        Verify no avatar icons are present
        Verify the share list button is displayed
        Tap the share list button
    Expected
        The share list button labeled "Del liste" is displayed in the share area
        No avatar icons are visible anywhere on the shopping list screen
        Tapping "Del liste" presents the share shopping list bottom sheet

        N/A

SHARE_LIST-21 | Successive retry failures do not degrade sheet state or responsiveness [P1]
    Preconditions
        User is logged in to the Rema 1000 app
        Share shopping list sheet is presented and the fetch who has access endpoint is configured to fail consistently
    Actions
        Wait for the initial fetch to fail and the error alert to appear
        Tap the retry button
        Wait for the second request to fail and the error alert to reappear
        Tap the retry button again
        Wait for the third request to fail and the error alert to reappear
        Tap the dismiss button
    Expected
        Each retry cycle follows the same sequence: alert is dismissed, sheet returns to loading state, a new request is fired, and the alert reappears on failure
        The error alert title and message remain accurate and correctly localized on every reappearance
        The share sheet does not crash, become unresponsive, or display corrupted content after multiple consecutive retry cycles
        Tapping the dismiss button after any number of retries successfully closes both the alert and the share sheet
        User returns to a stable and usable shopping list screen

        N/A
